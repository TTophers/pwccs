STUDENT LIFE

GROWING IN FAITH, FRIENDSHIP, AND PURPOSE

Life as an Eagle

Peace With Christ students are known, encouraged, and challenged each day. From classroom learning to chapel, service projects, field trips, and school traditions, student life is built to help children grow academically, socially, and spiritually.

Our school community gives students room to discover their gifts, build lasting friendships, and practice servant leadership in age-appropriate ways. Every student matters, and every moment is an opportunity to point one another back to Christ.

Student Opportunities

Faith Formation

Weekly chapel, classroom devotions, and mission outreach help students connect faith with daily life.

Community

Small-school relationships give students a place to belong, participate, and be supported by caring adults.

Activities

School events, service days, classroom celebrations, and enrichment activities make learning memorable.

Chapel & Faith Life

Weekly chapel services include worship, scripture, and messages from staff. Students also participate in Faith Families, building relationships across grade levels through service, prayer, devotions, and faith-centered activities.

Student Leadership

Student leadership opportunities give students the chance to lead, serve, and support the school community while developing confidence and compassion.

Safety Patrol

Helps younger students safely arrive at school and guides them to their classrooms, ensuring a welcoming and secure start to the day.

Reading Buddies

Pairs older students with younger peers to foster a love for reading, allowing mentors to practice leadership through patience, guidance, and encouragement.

Faith Families

Brings together multi-grade groups of students to complete servant events, giving older students the opportunity to guide and inspire younger peers through service.

Technology

Students develop the knowledge, character, and digital skills to thrive in an ever-changing world. Through our STEM program, digital citizenship, and hands-on learning experiences, students become creative thinkers, collaborative problem-solvers, and responsible digital citizens. With a 1:1 Chromebook program, technology is purposefully integrated into learning to prepare students to lead and serve in a technology-rich world.

Lunch Program

Peace With Christ participates in a lunch program providing meals five days a week at a cost to families. School Eatery posts monthly meal options for families to consider.

If families do not use the lunch program, lunches must be brought from home and should not require refrigeration. Nutritious meals are important for student health and learning. Microwave ovens are available only for Grades 4-8. Kindergarten through Grade 3 students should bring hot food in a thermos or bring a cold lunch.

Peace With Christ is unable to accommodate personalized meal deliveries. Food delivery services such as Uber Eats or DoorDash are not permitted. Students may bring a nutritious mid-morning snack from home. Candy, carbonated drinks, and gum are not permitted on school property.

Peace With Christ Christian School participates in the Federal School Milk Program. Students may purchase milk cards and choose from 2% white milk or fat-free chocolate milk.

Dress Code & Spirit Wear

Our standardized dress code promotes a respectful and focused learning environment. Spirit wear is available throughout the year, allowing students to represent their school with pride.

Field Day Fun

One of the most exciting days of the year, Field Day brings students together for games, friendly competition, and unforgettable memories. It’s a celebration of community, teamwork, and school spirit.
