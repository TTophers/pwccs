WELCOME

Peace With Christ Early Learning Center

Hello and welcome to the Peace With Christ Early Learning Center. Since 1995, our ELC has partnered with families in Aurora to nurture young children through loving care, hands-on play, kindergarten readiness, and the daily sharing of God's love.

WHY CHOOSE US?

A caring place to learn, play, and grow

Visit our school and you immediately see that students, parents, and staff together build an educational and faith community you will want to belong to. We look forward to encouraging your child as he or she works and plays with other children.

Our program offers a play based curriculum that invites children to explore their world in a hands-on way while building age-appropriate skills for kindergarten readiness.

Contact Janae Lato for more information: elc@pwclc.org or 303.699.8687

Philosophy of Education

We believe children, created in the image of God, must be nurtured in their whole being: mental, physical, emotional, spiritual, and creative. Loving, caring adults provide opportunities and safe environments for each child to grow.

Because children develop at different rates, our educators draw each child into varied forms of play so every child can find opportunities for growth and success.

And Best of All...

We treasure the opportunity to share the love of Jesus Christ every day. The faith values you are living out at home are mirrored throughout your child's day.
